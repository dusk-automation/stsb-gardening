Season by season

What your garden needs right now.

Albury Wodonga has frosty winters and hot, dry summers. Here is how we look after a garden through the year.

Spring

September to November

  • Mowing picks up again as lawns start growing fast
  • Weeding and fresh mulch before the weather warms up
  • Planting while the soil is warm and still moist
  • The first hedge and shrub trim of the season
Book a spring tidy-upFull garden care guide

Summer

December to February

  • Mowing a little higher so lawns cope with the heat
  • Keeping garden beds mulched to hold in moisture
  • Regular tidy-ups, including while you are away
  • Trimming back fast summer growth
Book summer mowingFull garden care guide

Autumn

March to May

  • Leaf and garden debris clean-ups
  • A good time to plant trees, shrubs and new turf
  • Pruning back summer growth
  • Getting garden beds ready for winter
Book an autumn clean-upFull garden care guide

Winter

June to August

  • Mowing slows down, so visits can be spread out
  • Pruning roses and deciduous plants
  • Tidying up after frost and wind
  • Planning a spring makeover
Plan a winter pruneFull garden care guide

A general guide. Every garden is different, so ask us about yours.
